# Authoritative Sources on Radiotherapy Quality and Risk Management

This document gathers core authoritative sources, scientific literature, and industry guidelines on radiotherapy patient quality, risk management methods (like FMEA/TG-100), and Incident Learning Systems (like RO-ILS).

---

## 1. AAPM Task Group 100 (TG-100) Report

The landmark publication that shifted the radiotherapy community from prescriptive machine-only checking to proactive workflow risk analysis.

*   **Title:** Application of risk analysis methods to radiation therapy quality management (AAPM Task Group 100 Report)
*   **Authors:** M. Saiful Huq, Bruce A. Fraass, Peter B. Dunscombe, John P. Gibbons Jr., et al.
*   **Journal/Publisher:** *Medical Physics* (July 2016)
*   **URL:** [https://aapm.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1118/1.4947547](https://aapm.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1118/1.4947547)
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **Process Mapping:** Detailing all clinical activities step-by-step from patient consult to treatment.
    *   **Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA):** Proactive evaluation of what can fail, scoring them on Severity (S), Occurrence (O), and Detectability (D) to calculate a Risk Priority Number ($RPN = S \times O \times D$).
    *   **Fault Tree Analysis (FTA):** Systematically analyzing the root causes of major clinical failures.
    *   **Customization:** Designing a tailored Quality Management (QM) plan based on specific clinical resources and technology rather than generic checklists.

## 2. Radiation Oncology Incident Learning System (RO-ILS)

The premier national database for recording and learning from safety events, near misses, and unsafe conditions in the United States.

*   **Title:** RO-ILS: Radiation Oncology Incident Learning System
*   **Sponsoring Bodies:** American Society for Radiation Oncology (ASTRO) & American Association of Physicists in Medicine (AAPM)
*   **Operations Partner:** Clarity Patient Safety Organization (PSO)
*   **URL:** [https://www.astro.org/Patient-Safety-Quality/RO-ILS](https://www.astro.org/Patient-Safety-Quality/RO-ILS)
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **Non-punitive Reporting:** Fosters a "just culture" where staff can log incidents without fear of blame.
    *   **Legal Protections:** Operates under the Patient Safety and Quality Improvement Act (PSQIA) of 2005, giving federal confidentiality and privilege protections.
    *   **RO-HAC Analysis:** The Radiation Oncology Healthcare Advisory Council analyzes aggregate national data to publish case studies, safety notices, and quarterly reports.

## 3. World Health Organization (WHO) Radiotherapy Risk Guidelines

International guidelines and training frameworks to ensure global patient safety in radiotherapy.

*   **Title:** Radiotherapy Risk Profile: Technical Manual
*   **Publisher:** World Health Organization (WHO)
*   **URL:** [https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241596954](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241596954)
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   Synthesizes international clinical data to identify the most common causes of accidental over- or under-exposures.
    *   Emphasizes double-checks, independent dosimetry validation, and robust communication during handovers.

## 4. International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) Safety Standards

Global safety reports, accident analyses, and preventive systems created by the IAEA to guide radiotherapy practices worldwide.

*   **Title:** Prevention of Accidental Exposure to Patients Undergoing Radiation Therapy
*   **Publisher:** International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) - Safety Reports Series No. 17
*   **URL:** [https://www.iaea.org/publications/6027/prevention-of-accidental-exposure-to-patients-undergoing-radiation-therapy](https://www.iaea.org/publications/6027/prevention-of-accidental-exposure-to-patients-undergoing-radiation-therapy)
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   Analyzes historical radiotherapy major accidents to extract systemic lessons.
    *   Recommends independent calibration, formalized clinical check lists, and proactive safety management systems (SEPAR - Safety Evaluation of Radiation Therapy).

## 5. Practical Clinical FMEA Implementation Literature

A notable study demonstrating how FMEA and risk prioritizations are integrated into modern radiotherapy departments.

*   **Title:** Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) in Radiotherapy: Practical Tools and System Analysis
*   **Journal:** *Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics*
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **High-Risk Stages:** Identifies contouring, treatment planning, data transfer (e.g., DICOM, couch-density corrections), and patient positioning as the highest-RPN categories.
    *   **Mitigation Actions:** Standardizing checklist templates, automating electronic data transfers, and establishing robust physical setups to reduce human error rates.
