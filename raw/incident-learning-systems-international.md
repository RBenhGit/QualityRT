# International Radiotherapy Incident Learning Systems

## Overview

Multiple national and international incident learning systems (ILS) exist for radiation oncology beyond RO-ILS (U.S.). These systems vary in scope (national vs. international), governance (society-sponsored vs. government-mandated), and legal protections.

---

## RO-ILS: 10-Year Report (2014–2024)

- **Title:** Operations and Impact of a Specialty-Specific National Incident Learning System: Ten Years of Radiation Oncology Incident Learning System (RO-ILS)
- **Authors:** Eric C Ford, Suzanne Evans, Bill Salter, et al.
- **Journal:** International Journal of Radiation Oncology • Biology • Physics
- **Year:** 2026 (published February 1, 2026)
- **PMID:** 41218661
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41218661/

### Key Statistics (as of end of 2024)
- **781 facilities** enrolled across diverse U.S. practice settings (private, community/academic, free-standing/hospital-based)
- **41,516 events** reported to the PSO — the largest volume compared to any other national or international aggregated ILS
- 24% of reported events were actual incidents reaching patients
- ~10% rated as potentially severe or critical
- Over 100 educational releases issued (case studies, safety notices, themed reports, webinars)

### Ongoing Challenges
- Data quantity and quality improvement needed
- Better information extraction from reports
- Expanded stakeholder engagement
- Long-term program sustainability

## ROSIS: Radiation Oncology Safety Information System (Europe)

- **Founded:** 2001
- **Governance:** Developed by European health professionals; supported by ESTRO (European Society for Radiotherapy and Oncology)
- **Scope:** International, web-based voluntary incident reporting
- **Notable:** One of the earliest shared incident learning systems in radiation oncology
- **First report:** Profiles of participants and first 1,074 incident reports — PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21087801/
- ROSIS preceded RO-ILS by 13 years and helped establish the conceptual framework for specialty-specific reporting

---

## SAFRON: Safety in Radiation Oncology (IAEA — Global)

- **Launched:** December 2012
- **Governance:** International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)
- **Scope:** International — open to facilities worldwide
- **Scale:** Over 50 registered medical facilities; over 1,300 incident reports
- **Developed by:** Some of the same personnel who created ROSIS
- **URL:** https://www.iaea.org/resources/rpop/resources/databases-and-learning-systems/safron
- **Covers:** Errors, near-misses, unsafe conditions across all radiotherapy modalities and countries
- **Note:** Serves as the global complement to national systems like RO-ILS (U.S.) and ROSIS (Europe)

---

## United Kingdom: NRLS (National Reporting and Learning System)

- **Governance:** National Health Service (NHS), administered nationally
- **Scope:** National; covers all patient safety incidents including radiotherapy
- **Note:** Not specialty-specific to radiation oncology; covers all medical incidents
- **Relationship to RO-ILS:** Less specialized; ASTRO and AAPM considered the U.S. NRLS equivalent insufficient for RT-specific learning — motivating the creation of RO-ILS

---

## Canada: CARS-PSO and NSIR-RT

- **CARS-PSO:** Canadian Adverse Radiation therapy Safety — Patient Safety Organization equivalent
- **NSIR-RT:** National System for Incident Reporting in Radiation Therapy
- **Developed following:** AAPM 2012 Work Group on Prevention of Errors report (same report that formed the basis for RO-ILS)
- Separate system from RO-ILS but conceptually aligned

---

## Australia and New Zealand

- **RANZCR:** Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Radiologists has explored centralized radiation oncology incident reporting
- **Status (as of 2022):** Initiatives underway to broaden national reporting; not yet a fully operational national system equivalent to RO-ILS
- **Reference:** RANZCR Inside News, June 2022: https://issuu.com/ranzcr/docs/inside_news_june_2022/s/16241410

## Comparative Summary

| System | Region | Launch | Governance | Scale (reports) | Legal Protection |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RO-ILS | USA | 2014 | ASTRO/AAPM | 41,516 | PSQIA federal protection |
| ROSIS | Europe | 2001 | ESTRO | ~1,074+ | Varies by country |
| SAFRON | Global | 2012 | IAEA | ~1,300+ | Varies by country |
| NRLS | UK | 2003 | NHS | All-specialty | NHS framework |
| NSIR-RT | Canada | ~2013 | National | Specialty-specific | Canadian PSO law |
| RANZCR | AUS/NZ | In development | RANZCR | National | TBD |
