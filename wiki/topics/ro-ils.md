# RO-ILS: Radiation Oncology Incident Learning System

**Type:** Entity
**Status:** developing
**Last updated:** 2026-05-24
**Sources:** [radiotherapy-sources]

## Summary
RO-ILS is the US national database for recording and learning from safety events, near misses, and unsafe conditions in radiation oncology. Co-sponsored by ASTRO and AAPM and operated by Clarity PSO, it provides federal legal protections under PSQIA to enable confidential, non-punitive reporting and aggregate analysis across institutions.

## Key Facts / Claims
- Co-sponsored by the American Society for Radiation Oncology (ASTRO) and the American Association of Physicists in Medicine (AAPM) [radiotherapy-sources]
- Operations partner: Clarity Patient Safety Organization (PSO) [radiotherapy-sources]
- Operates under the Patient Safety and Quality Improvement Act (PSQIA) of 2005, conferring federal confidentiality and legal privilege protections on reported data [radiotherapy-sources]
- Promotes a **just culture**: staff can report incidents and near misses without fear of punitive consequences [radiotherapy-sources]
- The Radiation Oncology Healthcare Advisory Council (RO-HAC) analyzes aggregate national data and publishes case studies, safety notices, and quarterly reports [radiotherapy-sources]
- Covers three categories: safety events, near misses, and unsafe conditions [radiotherapy-sources]
