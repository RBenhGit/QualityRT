## Summary

Obsidian is a markdown-based knowledge management application that renders `[[wiki-links]]` as a navigable graph, making it a natural viewer for LLM Wiki compiled artifacts. Community plugins extend it with AI and graph-analysis capabilities.

- Recommended by Karpathy as the tool for browsing and visualizing the compiled LLM Wiki [karpathy-llm-wiki-2026]
