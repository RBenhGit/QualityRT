## Summary
A curated bibliography of core authoritative sources on radiotherapy patient safety, quality management, and incident learning. Covers the AAPM TG-100 framework, international guidelines from WHO and IAEA, the US national incident learning system (RO-ILS), and practical FMEA implementation literature.

## Key Facts / Claims
- AAPM TG-100 (2016) is the landmark report shifting RT from prescriptive machine checking to proactive workflow risk analysis using FMEA and FTA [radiotherapy-sources]
- RO-ILS is the US national incident learning system for radiation oncology, co-sponsored by ASTRO and AAPM, operating under PSQIA federal legal protections [radiotherapy-sources]
- WHO's *Radiotherapy Risk Profile: Technical Manual* synthesizes international clinical data to identify common causes of accidental over- or under-exposures [radiotherapy-sources]
- IAEA Safety Reports Series No. 17 analyzes historical major RT accidents and recommends SEPAR (Safety Evaluation of Radiation Therapy) as a proactive safety management system [radiotherapy-sources]
- FMEA studies in RT identify contouring, treatment planning, data transfer, and patient positioning as the highest-risk workflow stages [radiotherapy-sources]
